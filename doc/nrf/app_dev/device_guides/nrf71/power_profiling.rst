Power profiling on nRF7120 DK
#############################

.. contents::
   :local:
   :depth: 2

You can use the `Power Profiler Kit II (PPK2)`_ with the nRF7120 DK to evaluate the power consumption of nRF71 Series devices in low-power modes.
For detailed instructions, see the :ref:`power_consumption_sample_measuring` section of the :ref:`power_consumption_sample` sample documentation.
