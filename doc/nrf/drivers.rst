Drivers
#######

Nordic Semiconductor provides a variety of drivers that are used in samples and applications in the |NCS|.
Drivers are libraries that follow Zephyr's :ref:`zephyr:device_model_api`.

Additional drivers are provided in `nrfx`_.

The following pages describe peripheral drivers, peripheral custom drivers, and other drivers available in the |NCS|.
